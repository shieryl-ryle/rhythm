# Visual direction — Rhythm

> Pitch-facing visual north star for the deck, video, and screens. Tokens come from
> [DESIGN.md](../DESIGN.md); this doc applies them to the pitch. It is not a design system.

## 1. Mood
Calm, warm, unhurried, honest. Reference image: morning light on a paper journal next to a phone
showing a softly lit contribution grid.

## 2. Palette (token names)
- `surface` backgrounds; `ink` titles and body.
- `primary` for the one highlight per slide.
- `accent` for "return" moments (the welcome-back beat, returns count).
- `grid-0`..`grid-3` and `grid-pause` only in the grid key visual.
- `danger` is not used in the deck.

## 3. Typography
`display` (Fraunces) for slide titles at ≥ 40 pt; `body` (Inter) at ≥ 24 pt. Readable on a
projector and in a 1080p video. Max 25 words per slide.

## 4. Layout & chrome
16:9; generous margins; one idea per slide. Phone screenshots in a simple device frame, max two
per slide, with the caption below. Small "Rhythm (working name)" wordmark bottom-left.

## 5. Key visual
**The grid that forgives:** a commit grid showing three bright weeks, a calm neutral gap (pauses look like any quiet day), and
today's tile lit again, with an `accent` marker at the return. Caption: "Coming back is progress."

## 6. Storyboard
| # | Slide / video beat | Visual | Message |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Problem | Job statement in large type | Life changes; routines stop fitting |
| 2 | Evidence | Seven quotes summarised as icons (6 friends + founder), labelled "convenience sample" | Real barriers, honest boundary |
| 3 | Insight | Hypothesis: benefits feel less noticeable than effort; returning feels overwhelming | What we're testing |
| 4 | Rhythm | The loop: choose → try → share → better next time | How it works |
| 5 | Demo | Live demo / video ([Demo script](../demo-script.md)) | It works |
| 6 | The grid that forgives | Key visual | No guilt by design |
| 7 | Responsible AI | "AI only rewords" + data-on-device diagram | Safety and governance |
| 8 | Path to pilot | Three payer options + validation plan | Commercial path |
| 9 | Ask | Team + next steps | Pilot partners |

## 7. Motion budget
One animation only: grid tiles lighting up on slide 6 (≤ 2 s). No other transitions.

## 8. Do / Don't
### Do
- Show real app screens with the "Demo data" banner visible.
- Label every hypothesis as a hypothesis.
### Don't
- Dense infrastructure slides; unreadable screenshots; unsourced numbers.
- Clinical claims or before/after health results.

## 9. Design decisions (closed)
| Decision | Why |
|---|---|
| No red anywhere in the deck | Matches the no-guilt principle (BR-007) |
| Grid as the key visual | Owner's commit-grid idea; instantly familiar |
