Lilt privacy policy

Last updated 21 November 2026

In short: Lilt keeps what you tell it on your phone. It has no account, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking, and it does not send your answers, plans or reflections anywhere.

What Lilt stores, and where

After you agree on the first screen, Lilt stores the things you choose to tell it: your starting-point answers, the times you are free, what you leave out, your plan, how each activity went, and what Lilt noticed (which you confirm). This information is stored only on your device. Nothing is stored before you agree.

What Lilt does not do

  • It does not ask you to create an account or sign in.
  • It does not collect data about you on our servers, because there is no server that receives it.
  • It does not include advertising, analytics or tracking tools, and it does not track you across other apps or websites.
  • It does not read your health records, location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone.
  • It does not sell or share your information.

Safety check on what you type

If you type words into a free-text box that could mean you are in difficulty, Lilt checks them against a short list kept inside the app, on your device, and shows a support message instead of a suggestion. The text is not sent anywhere.

Your control

  • Export: in Settings you can copy everything Lilt holds about you as readable text, to keep or paste where you choose.
  • Delete: in Settings you can delete everything Lilt holds. Deleting the app also removes it.

The web version

The browser version of Lilt is delivered as static files by Google Firebase Hosting. Like any website host, the host may keep standard technical logs (such as IP address and time of request) under Google's terms. Your answers still stay in your browser's storage on your device. Browser storage is not encrypted, so someone with access to your device could read it.

What Lilt is not

Lilt is a planner for physical activity. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat or give medical advice. If you are unwell, talk to a health professional. In an emergency, call your local emergency number.

Children

Lilt is made for adults and is not directed to children.
